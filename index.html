About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. Based in Chhattisgarh and working across India, the foundation focuses on creating social impact through education, welfare, sustainability, women empowerment, and skill development.

  • Mission: To create lasting social impact through collective action and meaningful community support.
  • Vision: To build a more inclusive, compassionate, and empowered society.
  • Focus Areas: Education, food and clothing support, animal welfare, women empowerment, environment, and internships.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project SEVA

This initiative supports underprivileged people by distributing meals and clothing to those in need.

Impact: More than 50,000 meals and clothing items have been distributed.

Project BACHPANSHALA

This project helps underprivileged children by supporting school education, digital literacy, and life skills training.

Impact: It helps bridge educational gaps and gives children better learning opportunities.

Project UDAAN

This project empowers women in rural communities through self-help groups, skill development, and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Impact: It promotes confidence, awareness, and financial independence among women.

Our Social Impact

InAmigos Foundation works to improve lives through service, education, empowerment, and sustainability. Its projects support children, women, animals, and underserved communities while also encouraging youth participation through internships and volunteering.

  • Supporting underprivileged communities with food, clothing, and care.
  • Promoting education, digital learning, and life skills for children.
  • Encouraging women empowerment, environmental awareness, and social responsibility.
